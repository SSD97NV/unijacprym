## Global boundary of the supplement

No file in this repository proves stack algebraicity, the existence of a good
moduli space, fixed-stack descent, scheme-theoretic-image descent, completion
or algebraization, component incidence, scalar-gerbe descent, or any
canonical-ring theorem from the global part of the thesis. The certificates
are checks of the explicit identities listed in the table, not independent
proofs of the complete thesis statements.
